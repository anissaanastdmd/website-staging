Cosmetic Care

A Smile You Feel Confident Sharing

Brighten, straighten, and enhance your smile

Whether you'd like a brighter shade, straighter teeth, or a more even smile, cosmetic dentistry can help you feel more confident in your appearance.

Our goal is to help you achieve the smile you want by recommending the treatment option best suited to your needs, oral health, and desired results.

Our Cosmetic Dentistry Services Include:

  • Clear aligners
  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Veneers, including no-prep veneers
Request Appointment Call (312) 842-1018

Orthodontic Clear Aligners

Our office offers clear aligner orthodontic treatment to help improve the alignment and appearance of your smile. For more complex orthodontic cases, we may recommend a referral to an orthodontist for specialized care. Depending on your individual needs and goals, alternative cosmetic options, such as no-prep veneers, may also be discussed when appropriate.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening helps brighten your smile by reducing stains and discoloration. We can discuss whitening options suited to your teeth, sensitivity, and desired results to help you achieve a brighter, refreshed appearance.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells cover the front of your teeth to improve their color, shape, and alignment. No-prep veneers may be an option for some patients as an alternative to orthodontic treatment.
