Restorative Care

Restoring Strength, Function, and Comfort

Repair, protect, and replace teeth

Decay, damage, and missing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and feel about your smile. Restorative treatments repair and protect your natural teeth, or replace teeth that have been lost.

We'll walk you through your options, explain what each treatment involves, and help you choose the approach that fits your needs. When a case calls for specialized care, we'll refer you to a trusted specialist.

Our Restorative Dentistry Services Include:

  • Dental emergencies
  • Fillings & crowns
  • Anterior root canals
  • Tooth extractions
  • Bridges, partial & full dentures
  • Implant restoration with custom crowns, bridges, or dentures

Emergency Care

Unexpected dental problems can't always wait. Call our office and we'll help as promptly as possible.

Dental Emergencies

Experiencing a toothache, dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or another unexpected dental concern? Our office can evaluate your symptoms and help determine the appropriate treatment. We aim to address urgent dental concerns as promptly as possible and help relieve discomfort while protecting your oral health.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, infected, or cannot be saved, an extraction may be recommended. We evaluate each tooth carefully and discuss your treatment options, including ways to restore or replace the tooth when appropriate.

Repairing Teeth

Treatments that repair damage and decay so you can keep your natural teeth.

Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings repair cavities and minor damage while blending naturally with your smile.

Dental Crowns

We offer a variety of dental crown options to meet your needs and preferences. From natural, tooth-colored crowns that blend seamlessly with your smile to classic gold crowns and even unique colors and designs, we can help you choose a crown that reflects your style. Whether you prefer a natural look or something more distinctive, our team is here to help you find the right option for your smile!

Root Canal Treatment

Our office provides root canal treatment for anterior (front) teeth. Root canals involving back teeth, such as premolars and molars, or cases that are more complex may require specialized care and will be referred to an endodontist (root canal specialist). Our priority is to ensure you receive the appropriate treatment for your specific needs and the best possible care.

Replacing Missing Teeth

Options to restore your smile, chewing ability, and everyday comfort.

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to supporting teeth or restorations. Bridges help restore your smile, improve chewing function, and maintain the appearance of your natural teeth.

Dentures & Partial Dentures

Dentures and partial dentures replace missing teeth to help restore your smile, improve chewing ability, and support everyday speech. Whether you need to replace several teeth or an entire arch, we can discuss options designed to meet your needs and improve comfort and function.

Dental Implant Restorations

Dental implants can support custom crowns or other restorations designed to replace missing teeth. We provide implant restoration services to help complete your smile with a natural-looking replacement that supports comfortable chewing and everyday function. Implant placement and restoration may be provided by different dental professionals.
