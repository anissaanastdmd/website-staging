Preventive Care for Every Stage of Life

Preventive care that keeps your smile healthy

Cleanings & Gum Care

Healthy gums are essential to a healthy smile. We offer a full range of cleanings, from routine preventive care to specialized treatment for gum disease.

Regular Dental Cleanings

Routine dental cleanings help remove plaque, tartar, and surface buildup that brushing and flossing alone may not eliminate. Regular cleanings help maintain healthy teeth and gums, prevent oral health problems, and keep your smile feeling fresh.

Gingivitis Treatment

Gingivitis is the precursor of gum disease that can cause redness, swelling, and bleeding gums. With professional dental cleanings, improved home care, and personalized guidance, gingivitis can often be reversed before it progresses to more advanced gum disease.

Deep Cleanings (Scaling and Root Planing)

Deep cleanings treat gum disease by removing plaque and tartar from below the gumline and smoothing the tooth roots to help the gums heal. This treatment helps reduce inflammation and gum pocket depth and helps prevent further damage to the supporting structures of your teeth.

Periodontal Maintenance

After treatment for gum disease, periodontal maintenance helps keep your gums healthy and prevent the condition from progressing. These specialized cleanings focus on areas affected by gum disease and are typically scheduled more frequently than routine cleanings, based on your individual needs.

Periodontal Laser Treatments

Laser-assisted periodontal treatments may be used as part of a gum disease treatment plan to target diseased or inflamed gum tissue and support periodontal care. Your dentist will determine whether laser treatment is appropriate for your condition and whether it should be combined with traditional periodontal therapy.

Dental Exams & X-Rays

Regular exams, with diagnostic X-rays when needed, let us check your teeth, gums, and mouth, spot concerns early, and plan any care you need.

Children's Dentistry

Children's Dentistry & Adolescent Cleanings

We welcome children and adolescents and help them build healthy dental habits from an early age. Age-appropriate cleanings, regular examinations, and diagnostic X-rays help monitor growing teeth, identify potential concerns, and keep developing smiles healthy.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are thin protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth. They help seal out food particles and bacteria from the deep grooves where cavities commonly develop, especially in children and teenagers.

Fluoride Varnish

Fluoride varnish is a protective coating applied to the teeth to strengthen enamel and help prevent cavities. It is especially beneficial for children who are still developing their brushing habits, though it may also be recommended for adults at increased risk of tooth decay.

Protective Appliances

Custom-fit appliances that protect your teeth and the results of your dental care.

Night Guards

Custom night guards help protect your teeth from the effects of grinding and clenching during sleep. They can help reduce excessive wear on teeth and protect dental restorations while providing a comfortable, personalized fit.

Orthodontic Retainers

Retainers help maintain your teeth's alignment after orthodontic treatment. Wearing your retainer as directed helps prevent teeth from gradually shifting and protects the results of your orthodontic care.
