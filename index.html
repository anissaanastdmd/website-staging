Comprehensive Dental Care, Rooted in Community

From preventive visits and restorative treatments to cosmetic dentistry and injectables, we offer care designed to support your oral health, function, and confidence.

General Dentistry

Preventive care that keeps your smile healthy

Regular examinations, cleanings, and gum care help maintain oral health and identify concerns early, with personalized oral hygiene education for patients of all ages.

  • Dental exams & cleanings
  • Gum disease treatment & periodontal care
  • Children's & adolescent dentistry
  • Sealants & fluoride varnish

Restorative Dentistry

Repair, protect, and replace teeth

Treatments that restore the shape, strength, appearance, and function of teeth affected by decay or damage, options to replace missing teeth, and prompt care for dental emergencies.

Our Story

From Patient to Practice Owner

Dr. Anissa Anast first came to this practice in 1998 as a five-year-old patient of Dr. Vincent Arrigo. Years later she joined the team as a dental assistant in high school and returned during summer breaks from college.

After earning her undergraduate degree from Illinois State University and graduating from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine - Illinois, she gained experience at other offices. When Dr. Arrigo began planning his retirement, he asked her to take over. In summer 2023, Dr. Anast purchased the practice, carrying forward the relationships and commitment to patient care that shaped her own experience.
