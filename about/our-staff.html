Dr. Anissa Anast, DMD

Owner Dentist

Dr. Anast has been dedicated to the dental field since 2012, and her journey to becoming a dentist was fueled by a lifelong passion for oral health. She graduated with her undergraduate degree from Illinois State University before attending Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, where she earned her dental degree. Having been a patient at this practice for many years, Dr. Anast was thrilled to take ownership in the summer of 2023, continuing a legacy of exceptional care. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her dog, Millie (who you may spot around the office), and her husband, Nick. Dr. Anast is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate care to each of her patients, just as she experienced herself growing up in the community.

  • Member of the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE)
  • Member of the Chicago Dental Society (CDS), Illinois State Dental Society (ISDS), and American Dental Association (ADA)
  • Undergraduate degree, Illinois State University
  • Dental degree, Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine - Illinois

Dr. Vincent Arrigo

Dentist

Dr. Arrigo has owned and operated his own dental practice since 1985, and over the years, he’s built a reputation not only for his excellent care but for his fun-loving, goofy personality that always puts a smile on everyone’s face. Although officially retired, Dr. Arrigo still enjoys popping into the office to hang out with patients and soak in the positive, welcoming vibes of the team. When he’s not at the office, you can find him relaxing in Florida or Wisconsin, enjoying the best of both worlds. His cheerful spirit and love for his patients continue to be a big part of our practice, even in retirement.
