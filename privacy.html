Privacy Policy

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Anissa Anast Dental Office ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit anissaanastdmd.github.io (the "Website"). It applies only to information collected through this Website.

About Your Health Information

How we use and protect the health information in your dental records is described in our separate Notice of Privacy Practices, as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). A copy is available at our office or by request.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, along with limited technical information that is collected automatically when you use the Website.

Information You Provide

When you use our contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Any details you include in your message

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit. This information is used to keep the Website secure and working properly.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to you and to provide our services.

We Use Your Information To

  1. Respond to your questions and messages
  2. Schedule and confirm appointments
  3. Contact you about your request by phone or email
  4. Maintain and improve the Website

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only as needed to operate the Website and respond to your requests, or when required by law. For example, our website contact form is processed by a third-party form service that delivers your submission to our office, and our office location map is provided by Google Maps.

We Never Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Please Don't Send Sensitive Health Information

Our website forms and email are not intended for sharing detailed medical or dental information. Please don't include sensitive health details, Social Security numbers, or payment information in a website form or email. Call our office instead, and we'll be glad to help.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Encrypted Connection

The Website is served over HTTPS, which encrypts information sent between your browser and the Website.

Limited Access

Only authorized members of our team use the information you submit, and only to respond to your request.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time with questions about the information you've shared through the Website.

Access

Ask what information you've submitted to us through the Website.

Correction

Ask us to update or correct your contact information.

Deletion

Ask us to delete website form submissions, subject to any records we are required to keep.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Some third-party services embedded on the Website, such as Google Maps, may set their own cookies or collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Services We Use

Service Purpose
Website hosting Delivers the Website to your browser
Form service Delivers contact form messages to our office
Google Maps Displays our office location
Google Fonts Provides the fonts used on the Website

Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Website. Parents or guardians should contact our office directly to schedule care for a child.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
