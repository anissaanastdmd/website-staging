Our Commitment to Accessibility

Anissa Anast Dental Office is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities.

1. Our Goal

We are working to make our website conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website and make improvements as we add new content.

2. Measures We Take

To support accessibility, we aim to:

  • Use clear headings and a consistent page structure
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Label form fields so they work with assistive technology
  • Maintain sufficient color contrast for text and buttons, and underline links within text
  • Support keyboard navigation throughout the website, including menus and our FAQ, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link
  • Let you pause content that moves on its own, and limit motion when your device is set to reduce motion
  • Design pages that adapt to different screen sizes and zoom levels
  • Test our pages with automated accessibility tools and manual keyboard checks when we make changes

3. Third-Party Content

Some content on our website is provided by third parties, such as the embedded Google Map, our online form service, our dental membership plan sign-up (provided by Curve Memberships), and our social media profiles. While we choose providers with accessibility in mind, we don't control their content and can't guarantee it fully meets accessibility standards.

4. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the website may not yet be fully accessible. If you have trouble accessing any content or feature, please let us know so we can fix it and provide the information you need in another way.

5. Accessibility at Our Office

If you have a disability or need an accommodation during your visit, please let us know when you schedule your appointment, and we'll do our best to make your visit comfortable.

6. Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. When contacting us, please describe the page and the problem you encountered, and we'll respond as soon as we can.
