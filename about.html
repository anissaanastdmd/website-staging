Our Story

From Patient to Practice Owner

Dr. Vincent Arrigo opened his dental practice in 1985. Known for his cheerful, playful personality and longstanding patient relationships, he built the friendly, family atmosphere our team continues today.

Dr. Anissa Anast first came to the practice in 1998 as a five-year-old patient of Dr. Arrigo. Years later she joined the team as a dental assistant in high school and returned during summer breaks from college.

After earning her undergraduate degree from Illinois State University and graduating from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine - Illinois, she gained experience at other offices. When Dr. Arrigo began planning his retirement, he asked her to take over. In summer 2023, Dr. Anast purchased the practice, carrying forward the relationships and commitment to patient care that shaped her own experience.

Although retired, Dr. Arrigo still stops by to visit patients and staff.
