Common Questions

A few of the questions we hear most often.

Yes! New patients are welcome. Please call our office and our front desk will be happy to schedule your visit. See how to request an appointment for what to expect.

We accept all PPO dental insurances. Even if our office is out of network with your specific plan, you are typically still able to use your dental benefits with us. Visit our Insurance page for details.

Being out of network does not mean you cannot use your insurance. It simply means our office does not have a contracted rate with your insurance company. Depending on your plan, out-of-network benefits should cover a portion of your treatment, with many diagnostic and preventative services being covered at 100%.

For all new patients, we begin with a comprehensive dental exam to evaluate your overall oral health and determine the best treatment plan for your needs. Updated dental X-rays may be taken if needed, and specific gum measurements will be recorded to help us determine which type of cleaning is most appropriate for you. See our New Patients page for more.
