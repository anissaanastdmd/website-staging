Yes! New patients are welcome. Please call our office and our front desk will be happy to schedule your visit. See how to request an appointment for what to expect.

We accept all PPO dental insurances. Even if our office is out of network with your specific plan, you are typically still able to use your dental benefits with us. Visit our Insurance page for details.

Being out of network does not mean you cannot use your insurance. It simply means our office does not have a contracted rate with your insurance company. Depending on your plan, out-of-network benefits should cover a portion of your treatment, with many diagnostic and preventative services being covered at 100%.

For all new patients, we begin with a comprehensive dental exam to evaluate your overall oral health and determine the best treatment plan for your needs. Updated dental X-rays may be taken if needed, and specific gum measurements will be recorded to help us determine which type of cleaning is most appropriate for you. See our New Patients page for more.

When you request an appointment, we'll need your name, phone number, date of birth, and email, and we'll ask you to fill out our new patient form. To help ensure a smooth and efficient visit, we kindly ask that all dental X-rays and any additional provided forms be emailed to our office before your scheduled appointment. Please also bring your dental insurance card.

Yes. We welcome children and adolescents and help them build healthy dental habits from an early age, with age-appropriate cleanings, regular exams, X-rays, dental sealants, and fluoride varnish. Learn more about our general dentistry services.

Most patients benefit from an exam and cleaning every six months. If you've been treated for gum disease, periodontal maintenance cleanings are typically scheduled more frequently, based on your individual needs.

Experiencing a toothache, dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or another unexpected dental concern? Call our office. We can evaluate your symptoms and aim to address urgent concerns as promptly as possible. If you have a serious injury or swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.

Our office provides root canal treatment for anterior (front) teeth. Root canals involving back teeth, such as premolars and molars, or cases that are more complex will be referred to an endodontist (root canal specialist). We also perform tooth extractions when a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, infected, or cannot be saved.

We provide implant restoration services, with custom crowns or other restorations that replace missing teeth. Implant placement and restoration may be provided by different dental professionals, and we'll help coordinate your care.

We offer a variety of crown options, from natural, tooth-colored crowns that blend seamlessly with your smile to classic gold crowns and even unique colors and designs. Our team will help you find the right option for your smile.

Yes. We offer professional teeth whitening and clear aligner orthodontic treatment. For more complex orthodontic cases, we may recommend a referral to an orthodontist, and alternatives such as no-prep veneers may also be discussed when appropriate. Learn more about our cosmetic dentistry services.

Dr. Anast is a member of the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE), and her training in facial esthetics complements our approach to personalized cosmetic care. Botox can soften expression lines and may also help with therapeutic concerns such as jaw muscle tension. Learn more about injectables and threads.
