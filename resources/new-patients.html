New Patients Welcome!

If you are a new patient, please call our office at (312) 842-1018 and our front desk will be happy to schedule your visit. See how to request an appointment for what to expect.

We look forward to welcoming you to our office!

Your First Visit: A Comprehensive New Patient Exam

For all new patients, we begin with a comprehensive dental exam to evaluate your overall oral health and determine the best treatment plan for your needs.

Comprehensive Exam

We evaluate your overall oral health and determine the best treatment plan for your needs.

Updated X-Rays

Updated dental X-rays may be taken during this visit if needed.

Gum Measurements

Specific gum measurements are recorded to assess your gum health and determine which type of cleaning is most appropriate for you, whether a routine preventive cleaning or a more specialized periodontal cleaning.

Our goal is to ensure you receive the proper care for your teeth and gums while helping you maintain a healthy smile for years to come!
